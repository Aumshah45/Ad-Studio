**Rejected hypotheses:**
- *"ΔE 18 is too lenient":* the ΔE of human fails (5.6–10.7) and human passes (2.4–13.5) overlap completely, so no
  threshold separates them.
- *"Unsure handling causes misses":* there are 0 unsure verdicts in v1 and 4 in v2, none on a disagreeing item.

**Calibration note:** every v1 product miss is on held-out P5. The calibration split (P1–P3) has one real product
failure, B12-final, so product fidelity cannot be calibrated against the real failure mode on P1–P3.

## Ranked fix proposals
1. **R1: human label pass (about 10 min, no system risk).**
   - (a) Fix the plant rows. **Done.**
   - (b) Give reason codes for the 19 v1 product and context fails, moving pure composition causes into composition.
   - (c) Rule on the text of B12-nat (v1), B13-nat and B16-nat, and on B06-nat. Projected: v2 text precision
     0.52 → 0.70 and v2 agreement 0.75 → 0.85.
   - (d) Decide whether a red P4 bottle counts as a product failure.
2. **R2: planted generator plant-3.** Blend mutations without seams, or leave their composition and context labels
   unasserted. Projected with R1a: v2 composition precision 0.35 → 1.00 and context 0.56 → 0.83. Low risk.
3. **R3: composition judge v2.**
   - Name the scene object whose real size is most certain and give sizes in cm; fail when the implied/expected ratio
     falls outside 0.6–1.6×.
   - List up to 3 signs the product was pasted in.

   Tune on B07–B10 and B12-final, and report B18 and B20 as held-out. Medium risk, about $0.10.
4. **R4: OCR hardening.**
   - Ignore stray tokens under 4 characters or with low confidence.
   - When the vision read-back matches exactly and OCR differs only by an accent, a missing trailing line or trailing
     junk, return unverified and re-OCR the zone at 2×.
   - Keep failing when both see an extra character; the 11 text plants must stay at 100%.

   Medium risk.
5. **R5: stray-text read-back ignores the product's own label strings.** Low risk.
6. **R6: context judge v3.** Any single listed contradiction is enough, and season cues must be dominant, not
   incidental. Add a "physically coherent scene" check only if R1b shows it as a distinct cause.
7. **R7: hue check.** Only if R1d says red fails. High risk: calibration has only one chromatic product.
8. **R8/R9: not recommended.** Turning box area into a failure is net negative on calibration, and no ΔE threshold
   separates the classes.

**Pipeline bug found:** the headline line-break marker ("/") can be rendered as text (v2 B13-nat). Fix it in the
prompt's line handling.
