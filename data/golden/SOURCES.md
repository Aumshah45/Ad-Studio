## Generated images (`outputs/`, `planted/`)
Generated by this project's pipeline with Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite Image / Flash Image (SynthID-watermarked by Google).
Planted failures are programmatic mutations of those outputs (seeds recorded in `planted/manifest.jsonl`).
